Does Insurance Cover Water Damage in Arizona?

The short answer: it depends on the cause. Here is how Arizona homeowners policies usually treat water damage — educational guidance, not legal or financial advice.

Home › Does Home Insurance Cover Water Damage in Arizona?

Quick answer: Most Arizona homeowners policies cover sudden, accidental water damage — such as a burst pipe or a water heater failure — but usually do not cover gradual damage or damage from neglect. Check your own policy and confirm coverage with your insurer.

Usually covered

  • Sudden, accidental damage — a burst pipe, a water heater failure, an appliance hose that lets go.
  • Damage from a sudden roof leak or storm event, depending on your policy.

Usually not covered

  • Gradual damage — a leak that went on for weeks or months unnoticed.
  • Damage from neglect or lack of maintenance, such as worn seals never replaced.
  • Damage from flooding may need separate flood insurance — check your policy.

How to protect your claim

  • Document everything with photos and video before cleanup, and keep a list of damaged items.
  • Call your insurer promptly and keep copies of every message, receipt, and report.
  • Do not throw damaged items away until your adjuster has seen them, unless they are a health hazard.
  • Ask us for moisture readings and drying logs — objective documentation your adjuster can use.

This page is educational only and is not legal or financial advice. Your policy wording decides what is covered.
