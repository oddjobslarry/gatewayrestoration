Our Services

Complete Water Damage Restoration Services

Gateway Restoration is Chandler's locally owned water damage restoration company, built around one simple idea: the faster a property is dried, the more of it can be saved. This services hub is the starting point for every homeowner, property manager, and business owner in Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Tempe, Queen Creek, and Phoenix who needs to understand exactly what water damage restoration involves, from the moment water is discovered to the final walkthrough after rebuild.

Every water loss follows the same general arc, even though the details change from a small supply-line leak under a kitchen sink to a full Category 3 sewage backup. Water mitigation comes first: stopping the source, extracting standing water, and putting equipment in place before damage spreads any further. Restoration follows, covering everything from structural drying to rebuilding drywall, flooring, and baseboards back to pre-loss condition. When storms or flash flooding are involved, flood cleanup adds an additional layer of debris removal, sanitization, and contamination control specific to monsoon season in the East Valley.

Drying is the technical core of the entire process. Our IICRC-trained crews use truck-mounted extraction, commercial air movers, and industrial dehumidifiers, all placed according to IICRC S500 drying calculations rather than guesswork. We back that up with moisture mapping and thermal imaging so hidden pockets of water inside walls, under flooring, and behind cabinetry don't get missed. Mold prevention is built into every job by design, not treated as an afterthought, because Arizona's warm indoor environments allow mold to begin colonizing damp materials within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure.

Finally, every job includes thorough insurance documentation: photos, moisture logs, and detailed reports written the way adjusters expect to see them, so your claim moves faster and with fewer disputes. We work directly with insurance carriers and can arrange direct billing with many of them, which means you're not fronting the full cost of restoration out of pocket while your claim is processed.

Water Classification

Category 1, 2 & 3 Water Cleanup

Every job starts with classifying the water, because contamination level determines which protective protocols and equipment are required.

Category 1 water is clean water from a supply line or appliance with no contamination risk. Category 2 water, often called gray water, contains some contaminants — think washing machine or dishwasher overflow — and carries a mild health risk if left untreated.

Category 3 water is grossly contaminated: sewage backups, flood water, or water that has sat long enough to grow bacteria. It requires full protective equipment, aggressive removal of affected porous materials, and thorough sanitization.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is water mitigation?

Water mitigation is the set of immediate actions taken to stop water damage from getting worse, including extraction, structural drying, and moisture control, performed before full restoration and rebuild work begins.

What is structural drying?

Structural drying removes moisture from a building's framing, drywall, subfloor, and other structural materials using commercial air movers and dehumidifiers, guided by IICRC drying calculations and verified with daily moisture readings.

How quickly should water damage be addressed?

Within the first few hours of discovery. Drywall, carpet padding, and subfloor begin absorbing moisture immediately, and mold can start developing within 24 to 48 hours in Arizona's warm indoor environments.

Will insurance cover water damage?

In most cases, yes, when the damage is sudden and accidental — a burst pipe, water heater failure, or storm intrusion. Damage from long-term neglect or a gradual leak is typically excluded. We help document your claim.

Can mold grow within 24 hours?

Yes. In Arizona's warm indoor temperatures, mold spores can begin colonizing damp materials within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, which is why fast extraction and drying matter so much.

What is Category 3 water?

Category 3 water is grossly contaminated water, such as sewage backups, flood water, or water that has sat long enough to grow bacteria. It requires full protective equipment and thorough sanitization.

Can belongings be saved?

Many belongings can be saved when treated quickly. We offer content pack-out, document drying, and contents cleaning to restore furniture, electronics, and papers whenever possible.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. Gateway Restoration provides 24/7 emergency water damage response across Chandler and the surrounding East Valley, every day of the year.

How long does restoration take?

Most residential structural drying takes three to five days. Larger losses, Category 3 water, or homes with dense materials like tile and concrete slab can take longer, with rebuild extending the overall timeline.

Do you serve commercial properties?

Yes. We provide commercial water damage restoration for offices, retail spaces, HOA common areas, and properties managed by property management companies throughout Chandler.
